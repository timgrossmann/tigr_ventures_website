ORBIT · One Tap Forest

Privacy policy

Effective date: 14 September 2026

ORBIT is a one-tap forest game by Tigr Ventures. This policy explains how information is handled in the current browser prototype, when you share a score or contact us, and when you visit this page.

Current version and planned release. ORBIT's mobile release is in development. The current prototype has no advertising SDK or consent SDK. A free mobile release supported by Google AdMob is planned. The advertising section explains that plan; it does not describe an integration that is already running. We will update this policy to match the verified release before enabling ads.

Game progress and preferences

The current game saves personal bests, the total number of cleared gaps, companion and forest choices, and music and sound preferences in your browser's local storage when it is available. Discoveries are determined from that progress. Daily trails use a date on your device.

You do not need an ORBIT account. The prototype has no game backend, cloud save, online leaderboard, chat, purchase flow or analytics SDK. It does not upload your local progress to Tigr Ventures. Music and effects are synthesized on your device.

Local progress remains until you clear the game's site data, your browser removes it, or the relevant browser profile is removed. You can delete it through your browser's site-data settings for the address where you play ORBIT. This also removes saved settings and bests; we have no server copy to restore. Browser or device backup features are controlled by your software and its settings. Storage and backup behavior for the planned native app must be checked before release.

Sharing a score

When you choose “Share score”, ORBIT creates a score image and challenge message locally. They can include your score, personal best, companion, forest and Daily trail date. You choose the destination through your device's sharing interface, or use the available save or copy option. The game does not share automatically or request access to your contacts.

When played from a public web address, the current sharing code can also include a challenge link containing course, companion and score-target parameters. Local previews omit that link. A shared link can reveal those details to recipients and the service hosting the link when opened. Shared images and messages are handled by the recipient and the app or service you choose under their own privacy policies.

Planned advertisements

We plan to offer ORBIT free with occasional Google AdMob advertisements between runs. Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform are not yet integrated into the prototype. No advertising identifier is requested and no ad request is sent by the current game.

Google's Mobile Ads data-disclosure guide describes possible collection of IP addresses, which can indicate general location; device identifiers; ad views and interactions; and crash and performance information. Depending on the release configuration, Google may use this information for advertising, measurement, analytics and service operation. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Before ads are enabled, we must verify the actual providers, data use, retention and international transfers, and publish the applicable legal bases and regional privacy choices here. The release must provide any required consent and privacy controls. Whether Apple's tracking permission is needed depends on the final tracking and identifier use. The current prototype has no ad privacy-options screen or tracking-permission prompt.

Fonts and this website

The current game prototype requests interface fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore connects to Google's servers and sends connection information, including your IP address and browser request details. Local progress is not part of those font requests. Google's handling of information is described in its Privacy Policy. This external font dependency, including its legal basis or replacement with bundled fonts, must be reviewed before the mobile release.

This privacy page uses local assets and system fonts. It contains no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Requests to view it expose your IP address and technical request information to the hosting service; GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub Pages documentation and the GitHub Privacy Statement for its retention and other processing practices.

Google and GitHub describe processing in the United States and other countries in their privacy policies, including their applicable transfer safeguards. GitHub describes use of EU standard contractual clauses. Contact us if you need further information about recipients or safeguards relevant to your use of ORBIT.

When you contact us

If you email tim@tigr.ventures, we receive your email address, your message and anything you choose to attach, such as device details or screenshots. We use this information to reply, investigate your request and handle follow-up questions. Providing this information is voluntary, but we may be unable to help without details of the issue. Please send only what is needed.

Correspondence is handled by Tigr Ventures and the email and technical service providers used to deliver and store it. We retain it for as long as needed to resolve the request and relevant follow-up, and where necessary to meet legal recordkeeping obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. The applicable period depends on the request and those obligations. You can ask us about or request deletion of correspondence using the contact above.

Legal bases and your rights

Where the GDPR applies, we handle support requests and provide a secure information website on the basis of our legitimate interests in responding to users and operating our services (Article 6(1)(f)). Processing necessary to fulfil a contract or take steps you request before a contract is based on Article 6(1)(b); legally required records are based on Article 6(1)(c).

Subject to the applicable conditions, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of personal data we hold about you. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Email tim@tigr.ventures; we may need information to verify and locate your request.

You may complain to a data protection authority, including the Unabhängiges Landeszentrum für Datenschutz Schleswig-Holstein, or the authority where you live or work. We do not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you through the current game.

Children's privacy

ORBIT is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. The current game does not ask for a name, age or account. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and handle it appropriately. Audience settings, ad content and any age-related requirements must be checked for the countries included in the mobile release.
